Methodology

Adjusted minutes: baseline minutes + optionally inferred recovery for historical foul/blowout anomalies, bounded for realism. Expected fantasy points: adjusted minutes × per-minute FP × matchup factor × usage factor. Ownership: user-supplied percentage; if absent, clearly marked synthetic demo estimates or blank defaults. Model score: projected points plus a scaled ownership preference; ceiling uses optional variability. Scores are research heuristics, not validated estimates of tournament value.

CSV columns: name,team,position,salary,minutes,fp_per_min,ownership,matchup_factor,usage_factor,foul_lost_min,blowout_lost_min,ceiling_sd,status. Use OUT to exclude an injured player. Position supports PG etc or PG/SG.
